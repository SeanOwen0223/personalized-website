South Korea

The Mecca of Esports

As an avid player and fan of League of Legends, visiting the T1 Tower in Seoul is the ultimate esports pilgrimage. T1 is the most historically dominant organization in LoL history, home to the legendary Demon King himself, Faker.

Going to the T1 headquarters isn't just about seeing a building; it's about connecting with the competitive spirit, the history of the world championships, and the peak of gaming culture that South Korea is famously known for. It represents the pinnacle of gaming achievement.
