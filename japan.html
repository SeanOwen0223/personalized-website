Tokyo, Japan

The City of Neon Lights and Culture

Tokyo is the ultimate intersection of deeply rooted traditional culture and bleeding-edge technology. As an IT student who appreciates gaming and anime, walking through districts like Akihabara or experiencing the organized chaos of the Shibuya Crossing is a bucket-list dream.

Beyond the tech and pop culture, I want to experience the authentic Japanese culinary scene, the serene temples tucked away in bustling cityscapes, and the unparalleled discipline and cleanliness of the country. It is a place that feels like stepping into the future while honoring the past.
