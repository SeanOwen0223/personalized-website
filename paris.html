Eiffel Tower, Paris

The City of Light and Art

Paris is a city that needs no introduction, and the Eiffel Tower stands as its grand centerpiece. There is something undeniably magical about the architecture, the history, and the romantic atmosphere that surrounds it.

Visiting Paris is on my list because it offers an entirely different vibe from my other destinations—it's focused on classical art, historic European architecture, cozy cafes, and breathtaking golden hour views. Watching the tower sparkle at night is an iconic experience that I look forward to capturing.
